Who We Are?
More Than a Technology Supplier. Your Reliable IT Foundation.
UBS Establishment is a high-tech systems integrator and one of Kazakhstan’s largest multi-vendor companies. With more than 30 years of experience in the Information Technology (IT) sector, we are one of the leading players in the market, specializing in the supply of IT equipment and solutions from the world’s leading brands, including:
HP, HPE, Lenovo, Dell, Cisco, APC, Xerox, Eaton, NetApp, Hikvision, Poly, Aruba, and many others.

We also provide a comprehensive range of licensed software solutions from leading technology partners, including Microsoft, AutoCAD, VMware, Veeam, ESET, and Kaspersky.
We are recognized for our strong financial stability, fast and efficient order fulfillment, and high levels of customer satisfaction, supported by a highly qualified and dedicated technical team.

Today, UBS Establishment is a trusted supplier of IT and office equipment to a large number of companies operating in Kazakhstan’s oil and gas, banking, industrial, and international sectors.
UBS is certified under the following management systems:
  • ISO / ST RK Quality Management System
  • ST RK ISO Environmental Management System
  • ISO/IEC Information Security Management System
